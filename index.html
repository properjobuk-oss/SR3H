Research

We test how AI uses information, works with people and supports decisions.

Output and relevance

We test how context and sources affect AI answers, and how those answers can be checked.

Personal context and privacy

We investigate on-device processing, selective sharing and controls over what AI remembers and uses.

Tools and human control

We test which tasks suit automation, fixed rules or human review.

Discovery and recommendations

We study how AI finds and recommends businesses, and how sources and commercial influence affect its answers.
