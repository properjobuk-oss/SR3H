Human–AI systems engineering

Making capable models work in real systems.

A capable model is only one part of a dependable system. We work on the surrounding layer: capturing intent, selecting relevant context, scoping memory, authorising tools, preserving state and producing evidence for what happened.

The goal is to turn model capability into coherent, accountable work over time — without obscuring human judgement or exposing information the task does not require.

Research systems

Persistent context. Deliberate boundaries.

Conchup and Brane explore two sides of the same problem: how useful context can persist across complex work while remaining bounded, inspectable and under human control.

Persistence for the work

Conchup

Conchup maintains persistent engineering context across requirements, decisions, design, implementation, tests and release evidence.

It is designed to trace the impact of a change through affected work, preserving what changed, why it changed and the evidence that the resulting system remains correct.

Discuss Conchup

Context controlled by the person

Brane

Brane explores how people can provide AI with relevant, persistent context while keeping data, roles and permissions under their control.

Rather than one undifferentiated memory, work, private life and other contexts can remain deliberately separate.

What does this system need to know for this task — and what does it not need to know?

How we work

Quietly capable.

  • Start with the real problem. Build the smallest coherent thing that materially improves it.
  • Make every layer agree. Language, interaction, design and engineering should describe the same product.
  • Keep context relevant and boundaries explicit. More information is not automatically better information.
  • Preserve human judgement and show evidence. Important decisions should remain understandable and checkable.
