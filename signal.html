What Signal measures

Each result starts with a recorded answer.

Signal shows the observation, sample size and limits. It does not turn a small test into a visibility score.

Presence

Absent, cited, mentioned or recommended.

Record how the business appears for each defined question.

Conditions

Keep the test conditions.

Store the question, AI system, model, date, sources and full answer.

Change

Repeat before drawing a conclusion.

Compare like-for-like runs and show when evidence is incomplete or test conditions changed.

A result is an observation from a defined test. It is not a market ranking or proof that a change caused an outcome.

Free website check

Start with the public website.

Check whether AI and search systems can access and understand the site. Signal inspects the page, robots rules, sitemap, metadata, structured data and public service information. When available, it adds a small dated search sample.

Website readiness and observed inclusion are reported separately.
