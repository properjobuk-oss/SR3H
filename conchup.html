Conchup · Persistent engineering context

Software engineering that keeps its reasoning.

Conchup is a workspace for complex software projects. It keeps requirements, decisions, implementation, tests and evidence connected, so a product can change without losing why it was built that way.

Discuss Conchup Back to selected work

From requirements to evidence

A clear record of what was built and why.

Work begins with agreed requirements, not generated code. Conchup turns those requirements into structured product, design and engineering work, then keeps each decision linked to the tests and evidence that support it.

Requirements

Changes stay connected.

When a requirement changes, the affected journeys, decisions and checks remain visible.

Decisions

The reasoning stays traceable.

The record shows what was agreed, why it changed and what evidence supports the current state.

Evidence

Progress has a clear meaning.

Proposed, implemented, tested and ready for release are kept separate.

What we are building towards

A governed route from a changed requirement to a reviewed build.

Conchup is being developed to assess changed requirements, rebuild the affected parts and return the result with evidence for human review before release.
