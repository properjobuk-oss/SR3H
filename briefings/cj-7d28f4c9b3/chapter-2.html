The customer relationship is changing

The customer relationship is moving beyond the supplier’s website.

→→02

Customers can explore a problem, develop a decision and interact with businesses within a chosen AI (Artificial intelligence) environment, sometimes before a supplier knows that an opportunity exists.

Today, a customer may move among search, supplier websites, portals, email and phone. A connected assistant can bring parts of that work into a continuing conversation. Search and supplier websites will also keep evolving.

02.02

Three reasons to stay

Continuity: retain useful context where the system supports it and the user permits it. Convenience: research, comparison and authorised actions may be available in one place. Intelligence: combine general reasoning with approved specialist knowledge and current business information.

Leaving the assistant does not necessarily mean losing intelligence: a supplier website may use equivalent models. The practical friction may be repeating accumulated context or interrupting the workflow.

02.03

Chosen does not mean controlled

Memory and personalisation depend on settings, access and platform rules. Familiarity or feeling understood does not establish correctness, better decisions or durable loyalty. Paid access does not by itself establish freedom from advertising or commercial influence.

A study of ChatGPT consumer use found practical guidance, information seeking and writing to be prominent activities. That supports a broader view of use than search alone; it does not prove improved decisions or reveal any individual customer’s private conversations.

THE THOUGHT TO TAKE FORWARD

Businesses have spent decades persuading customers to come to them. In an AI-mediated economy, businesses may increasingly need to go to the customer’s AI.
