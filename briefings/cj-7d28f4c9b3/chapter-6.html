SR3H’s response

IN DEVELOPMENT · Pre-revenue. Commercial model unproven.

Building the services for an AI-mediated world.

→→06

A changing world creates an opportunity for individuals and businesses to participate on better terms. SR3H’s proposition is to help them take control of how they are discovered, understood and able to interact in an AI-mediated world.

This is a developing commercial proposition. Products and working technology are at differing stages; intended capabilities must be separated from demonstrated functions.

For individuals · In development

MyLegend

Your identity. Your rules. Your agent.

Explore this idea

Personal identity, an AI-ready presence and authorised interaction across Discover, Understand and Do Business. People choose what to share and which services to connect. This does not promise absolute control over third-party AI (Artificial intelligence).

For organisations · In development

MyLegend for Business

Be discoverable. Be understandable. Be ready to do business.

Explore this idea

Approved business information, structured representation and supported commercial workflows across Discover, Understand and Do Business. Plugins, MCP (Model Context Protocol) and agent connections are options where appropriate; each capability needs to be demonstrated.

Commercial intelligence · Enabling capability

Signal

Understand how AI sees your market.

Explore this idea

Study how AI systems discover, describe and recommend businesses. Compare commercial query responses, investigate gaps and test changes. Signal informs both MyLegend services and may have standalone value. It does not access private customer conversations.

06.01

What we have built

SR3H’s experience is that a small team can conceive, specify and build sophisticated AI-native products quickly. Mandate and ConchUp support requirements, implementation and verification; supporting projects can provide selective demonstrations.

Working demonstrations show particular capabilities; they do not establish production readiness.

06.03

A founder’s experience

I’ve spent much of my professional life building technology businesses. A year ago, if you’d told me that I could personally develop in a week software that would previously have required a team of six to ten people, six to nine months and hundreds of thousands of pounds, I wouldn’t have believed you.

Yet that is what I’ve experienced.

AI has fundamentally changed the economics of the activity on which I’ve built much of my career. I didn’t predict that change, and I certainly don’t pretend to know exactly how AI will transform other industries.

But having experienced it firsthand, I find it difficult to be complacent about what might happen next.

Stephen

Personal account, not an independently audited benchmark.

06.04

Our next challenge

Validate with real customers. Develop distribution in an AI-mediated environment. Identify repeatable commercial use cases. Build revenue models and iterate from evidence.

The working thesis is that falling software production costs increase the importance of distribution, trust and repeatable adoption. Integration, security, reliable information, operations, accountability and specialist expertise remain valuable and can remain costly. Distribution is not automatically a durable advantage.

06.05

Our belief

Uncertainty about how a transformation will unfold is not a good reason to dismiss the possibility that it will be profound.

What do you see in your own business? Where does this thesis fit, where does it fail, and what might be worth testing together?
