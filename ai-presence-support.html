AIDO Discoverability Check

Support

Last updated 12 September 2026

AIDO Discoverability Check is read-only. The website form inspects a public business website and, when available, runs a small dated search sample. The ChatGPT MCP inspects the site without using SR3H's OpenAI API, then can prepare questions for ChatGPT to research with tools available in the user's session. Both report what was observed, not a ranking or commercial promise.

Get help

Email hello@sr3h.uk. Include the public website URL, what happened and the approximate time of the check.

Do not send passwords, access tokens, private URLs or other confidential information.

Common results

  • A private, local or credential-bearing URL is rejected by design.
  • A slow, oversized or non-HTML response may not be checked.
  • A result describes what was observed at that time and may change when the website changes.
  • If the website form's live discovery sample is unavailable or rate-limited, its technical check can still complete.
  • If ChatGPT research tools are unavailable, the MCP can provide the question pack but cannot complete the discovery stage.
